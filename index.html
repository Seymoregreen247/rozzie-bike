Why the V-neck costs more

      
It's a different shirt, not just a different collar. The ladies' V-neck is a tailored fine-jersey tee in soft combed cotton, and the V itself has to be cut, bound and stitched to lie flat. That extra fabric quality and sewing makes the blank cost more from the mill, so it costs us more to buy. The fitted body also means careful print placement on a narrower chest. You're paying for a better shirt, not a markup.
